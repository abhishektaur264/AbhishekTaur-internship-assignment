## 🎬 Proven Recording Tips for High Marks

1. **Rehearsal:** Read through the script twice out loud with a timer before recording. You will naturally land right between **4:45 and 5:00**.
2. **Tone of Voice:** Speak with a calm, warm, and confident cadence. Pretend you are speaking to a respected psychologist over a coffee chat.
3. **Browser Setup:**
   - Have `http://localhost:3001` loaded.
   - Close all unnecessary browser tabs and bookmarks bar (`Ctrl+Shift+B` in Chrome) for a clean, sleek screen.
   - When switching to mobile, press `F12` and click the Device Toggle icon (`Ctrl+Shift+M`), or simply narrow the browser window smoothly.
4. **Interaction Moments:**
   - Hover over the service cards and click **"Learn More"** to show the expansion.
   - Click the **"Book a Free Consultation"** button in the header or hero to showcase the interactive popup modal.
   - Click on the first FAQ item to show how smoothly it opens and closes.
